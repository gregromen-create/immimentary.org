## Main prompt

Photorealistic cinematic documentary footage, slow dolly shot through a beautiful modern documentary studio in Brooklyn. Warm tungsten key light, soft haze, polished concrete floor with gentle reflections, warm wood slat walls, tall frosted window light panels, a softly glowing neon wordmark "Immimentary" on the back wall. In the center, two cognac leather armchairs face each other on a deep red Persian rug, a small wooden side table with a glass of tea between them. A cinema camera on a tripod in the foreground, out of focus. Diverse people of different ages and backgrounds walk calmly through the studio at natural pace, crossing in both directions, some carrying a coat or a small vintage suitcase, one older man pauses and smiles at the chairs. Real people, natural skin texture, no actors posing, no text overlays, no logos besides the wordmark. Mood: warm, dignified, hopeful. Color palette: deep browns, amber, brick red, a touch of green.

## Shot list (if you need 3 × 10 s)

1. **0–10 s.** Wide shot, slow dolly left to right. People enter from both sides and walk past the two armchairs. Neon wordmark visible.
2. **10–20 s.** Medium shot at chest height, shallow depth of field. A woman with a small suitcase walks toward the guest chair; a young man passes in front, slightly blurred.
3. **20–30 s.** Slow push-in on the two empty chairs as the last person walks out of frame. The studio light warms. End on the chairs, ready for the next story.

Negative prompt: cartoon, CGI look, distorted hands, extra limbs, warped faces, text, subtitles, watermark, fast motion, shaky camera.
